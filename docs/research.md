# 🔍 Research

What already exists, and why git-campfire is still worth building. Research done in November 2026.

**Bottom line:** the basic idea ("a pixel campfire that grows with your streak") **already exists**.
git-campfire only makes sense if it stands out on **scene, atmosphere, visual quality and real data living
inside the scene**.

## What already exists

| Project | What it does | How git-campfire differs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **questlog-github-stats** (questlog.cc) | Medieval pixel-art cards: a character sheet, a book of languages and a `/streak` card with a campfire that grows with the streak. Runs on its own server, no token or workflow, with 16 day and night scenes. | The closest competitor. Those are static *cards*; git-campfire is an animated scene with atmosphere. |
| **lgtm.md** | Pixel-art widgets made by an Action. Its `flame` widget grows in 4 steps (<7, 7+, 30+, a blue heart from 100). | Output is a GIF. git-campfire is a crisp SVG animated with CSS, and a full scene. |
| **YourTomo / Github Pet** | A pixel cat with a state machine. A campfire appears when the streak is 3+ days. | The fire is an accessory, not the protagonist. |
| **devtrack** (badge) | A pixel flame that flickers and turns gray at streak 0. | A small badge. |
| **github-readme-streak-stats** | The classic: a fire icon with the current and longest streak. | Not pixel art, not a scene. |
| Git Bonsai, git-bloom-activity, GitTulkun, GitWorld Engine, snk | The genre of "something that grows or moves with your commits". | Crowded: the way to win is aesthetics and concept. |

The name `git-campfire` is free as a widget. The only matches are old hooks for Basecamp's Campfire chat.

## What makes git-campfire different

1. **A scene, not a card:** ruins, a cathedral or the void of space, with a character of its own.
2. **The data lives inside the scene:** the cathedral candles are your last 7 days, the menhir rune lights up, the embers fall into the black hole.
3. **Pure SVG animated with CSS,** no server and no GIF: light and crisp at any size.
4. **Themes with a strong identity** instead of color variations.
5. **A shared brand** with [GitFarm](https://github.com/FelipeBeltranING/gitfarm) and the upcoming Commit Horizon.
